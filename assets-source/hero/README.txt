Requirements:
  - PORTRAIT or square. The hero frame is 4:5 tall, so wide landscape
    photos will get their sides cut off.
  - at least 1200 x 1500px, ideally 1600 x 2000px
  - subject roughly centred, head not tight to the top edge
  - pick 5 that look like a set — similar lighting and mood.
    Mismatched photos make the rotation feel broken rather than designed.

Note: a glass panel sits over the BOTTOM of this image on the live site,
so avoid photos where the important detail is in the lower third.

Do not crop, resize or convert. That is handled automatically.
